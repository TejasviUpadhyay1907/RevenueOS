# Problem Statement

Merchants lose revenue every day through failed payments, checkout abandonment, subscription payment failures, overdue invoices and payment degradation. Existing systems often solve individual parts of this problem using predefined retry or communication workflows. Merchants therefore lack a unified intelligence layer that can determine which revenue is worth recovering, understand why it is at risk, predict recoverability, select the highest-value intervention, execute it safely, verify the outcome, and measure the incremental revenue created.

Short version:

> Merchants know when money is lost. They don't know which lost money is worth recovering, what action will recover it, or when they should stop.
